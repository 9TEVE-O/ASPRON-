# Client demo script — ASPRON Capsule 001 Safe Summary Gate

## Opening

This is not a compliance claim or production security system. It is a browser-only proof showing one control: raw, unreviewed, or unapproved content is blocked before any AI-style summary path can run.

## Demo flow

1. Show the fake intake record.
2. Click risk classification.
3. Attempt raw AI access and show the policy block.
4. Create the redacted copy.
5. Try to explain that summary is still blocked until review and exact AI input display are complete.
6. Approve the redacted copy as a human reviewer.
7. Display the exact AI-visible input before summary.
8. Generate the safe summary from the approved redacted copy only.
9. Generate the audit receipt.
10. Dissolve the capsule and show that the working capability is revoked while evidence remains.

## Buyer message

The point is not that the redaction patterns are perfect. The point is the control pattern: before AI touches sensitive intake, the system creates a safer copy, requires approval, shows exactly what AI would see, records evidence, and revokes the temporary capability.

## What not to claim

- Do not claim compliance.
- Do not claim secure deletion.
- Do not claim production-grade PII detection.
- Do not claim tamper-proof audit.
- Do not paste real client material into the demo.
